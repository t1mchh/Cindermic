# App icon

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. The full-resolution asset is `public/app-icon.png`; web favicons are resized derivatives. iOS uses the opaque 1024-pixel master and lets the operating system apply its mask.

## Prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: production iOS and desktop app icon for Cindermic, a wireless microphone utility. Generate one striking, polished square icon, full bleed opaque 1024x1024. Electric chartreuse lime background (#e1fa36) with a subtle luminous satin finish, NOT a black tile. Center a bold sculptural graphite microphone mark whose rounded capsule and short wireless signal arcs form a distinctive C-like silhouette. Minimal, confident, beautifully balanced. Small refined violet accent on one signal arc. Slight dimensional depth and soft contained shadow, crisp contours, superb readability at tiny app-icon size. The central mark should occupy roughly 65 percent of the canvas. No letters, words, watermark, surrounding phone, mockup, frame or baked rounded outer corners. All four corners should be filled with lime; iOS supplies the final mask.
